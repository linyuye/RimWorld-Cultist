# v0.9.4 · 密教服装与防剿局外观

## 简体中文

- 重绘八套相性服装和蛾相面具，调整五种成人体型的正面、侧面和背面。蛾相有了敞襟斗篷，启相有了紫边仪式袍。
- 四位具名追随者获得专属外观，防剿局猎人会以不同造型出现。
- 异象联动加入更多来信。发现实体、研读异象、举行心灵仪式或遭遇异象袭击时，有机会收到来信。
- 可在 Mod 设置中切换自制服装贴图和异象联动来信。

## English

- Updated the worn sprites for eight Aspect outfits and the Moth mask, with front, side, and back views fitted to all five adult body types. Moth gets an open-front cloak, and Knock gets a purple-trimmed ritual robe.
- Four named followers have distinctive appearances, and Suppression Bureau hunters now arrive in a variety of looks.
- Added more Anomaly crossover letters. They can appear after entity discoveries, studies, psychic rituals, or Anomaly raids.
- Custom apparel textures and Anomaly crossover letters can be toggled in Mod Settings.
